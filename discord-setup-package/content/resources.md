# Resources In The Cabinet

This server is documentary/archive space. It is not crisis care, medical advice, a plug board, or instructions.

If someone is in immediate danger, call local emergency services.

US support:
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988.
- SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-HELP / 1-800-662-4357.

House use:
- Put heavy story context in heavy rooms.
- Do not post sourcing, instructions, threats, doxxing, or private material.
- If something is genuinely risky, take it to staff instead of making the room carry it.

The project can show the real mess without turning the server into the mess.
