# Grimey House Rules

This house is allowed to be raw. It is not built to be polished, corporate, or fake-safe. It is built for real people, real stories, and the documentary archive.

The vibe is loose. The hard lines are not.

Hard lines:
- No sourcing, selling, plugs, middleman behavior, or hookup requests.
- No instructions for drug use, violence, weapons, evasion, or unsafe imitation.
- No threats, doxxing, stalking, harassment, or revenge posting.
- No minors, sexualized minors, exploitation, or reposting private material.
- No spam floods, scam links, malware, or impersonation.
- Heavy material goes in heavy rooms.
- The project can document danger without encouraging people to copy it.

Mods can clean risky stuff. That is not about making the room sterile. It is about keeping the house standing.
