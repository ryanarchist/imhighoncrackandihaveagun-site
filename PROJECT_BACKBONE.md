## Research-Informed Public Framing

Use the strongest, most defensible version of the theory.

Thread Theory:

- Threads are a model for recurring relational patterns.
- They can connect footage, memory, objects, rooms, platforms, audience reaction, and social systems.
- Do not claim that physics proves private thoughts directly control external reality.
- Clean wording: reality is experienced through recurring patterns that cross, stabilize, return, and reshape meaning.

Crack Capitalism:

- The strongest claim is structural.
- Addiction science supports reward loops, conditioned cues, escalating pursuit, and wanting without satisfaction.
- Capitalism can publicly reward endless accumulation while pathologizing similar compulsive loops at street level.
- Do not claim billionaires have the same proven brain scans as cocaine users.

## Security And Privacy Rules

The project can be radical. The infrastructure should be boring.

Default security stance:

- Remove personal payment rails from public pages where possible.
- Use a business-safe checkout provider for products and preorders.
- Use a separate creator email instead of private personal email.
- Use strong 2FA on every creator account.
- Back up website, video masters, captions, thumbnails, transcripts, and email list.
- Blur or redact third-party faces unless consent exists.
- Remove private addresses, license plates, medical documents, and unnecessary location metadata.
- Do not title videos like crime confessions.
- Do not frame unsafe behavior as a challenge.
- Do not publish hospital/location/legal specifics unless Ryan explicitly approves the exact public wording.
