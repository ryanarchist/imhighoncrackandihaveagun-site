Why I built
Modern Recovery Alliance.

Alongside the book and documentary, I’m building Modern Recovery Alliance (MRA). I started it because too many people feel they have nowhere to belong unless they follow one particular version of recovery. I want a community where people facing addiction, alcoholism, mental health struggles, PTSD, trauma or other health issues can help each other build better lives.

You don’t have to be sober to start. You get to decide what you want to work on. That might mean abstinence, taking prescribed medication, reducing harm, getting to an appointment or finding some stability. Science and modern medicine belong alongside mutual aid and lived experience. Your health, safety and relationships matter. Your rank is not measured by days.

Different paths.

Choose the changes you want to work toward.

Same humanity.

Belonging should not depend on following one particular version of recovery.

Build it together.

Bring your experience, questions and ideas into what comes next.

This project is possible because of Ellie, who passed away on November 15, 2022. I want the positive energy she brought into this world to keep helping people through what we build.

We’re still at the beginning. I may have founded MRA, but I want us to build and shape it together. This is ours. Have a look at what we’re building. Question it, bring your own experience, and help us work out what this community should become.

MRA is a community in formation. Peer meetings and support services have not opened yet. Explore the MRA website for the current project, resources and app preview.
