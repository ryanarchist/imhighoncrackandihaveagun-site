About

This Is Not A Recovery Commercial

IM HIGH ON CRACK AND I HAVE A GUN is a self-documented addiction archive by Ryan Homanics. It began as footage, notes, songs, screenshots, captions, and writing from inside a collapse. It became a book, a raw documentary, a soundtrack, a map, a Trap Pass system, and a live community.
